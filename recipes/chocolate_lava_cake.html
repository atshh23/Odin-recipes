Chocolate Lava Cake Recipe

Description

Chocolate lava cake is a rich and indulgent dessert with a soft chocolate sponge exterior and a warm, gooey molten chocolate center. When cut open, the “lava” flows out, making it a popular restaurant-style dessert that’s surprisingly easy to make at home.

Ingredients

For the Cake Batter

  • 100g dark chocolate (good quality)
  • 100g unsalted butter
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 50g sugar (about ¼ cup)
  • 30g all-purpose flour (about ¼ cup minus 1 tbsp)
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • A pinch of salt

For Greasing

  • Butter (to coat ramekins)
  • Cocoa powder or flour (for dusting)

Optional Serving

  • Cocoa powder or flour (for dusting)
  • Fresh berries
  • Powdered sugar

Preparation Steps

1.Prepare the Ramekins

  1. Grease 2–4 small ramekins with butter.
  2. Dust lightly with cocoa powder (or flour), then tap out excess.
  3. This helps the cakes release easily after baking.

2.Melt Chocolate and Butter

  1. In another bowl, whisk eggs, egg yolks, and sugar until light and slightly thick.
  2. Add vanilla extract and mix.
  3. Slowly fold in the melted chocolate mixture.
  4. Gently add flour and salt, mixing until just combined (don’t overmix).

3.Make the Batter

  1. In another bowl, whisk eggs, egg yolks, and sugar until light and slightly thick.
  2. Add vanilla extract and mix.
  3. Slowly fold in the melted chocolate mixture.
  4. Gently add flour and salt, mixing until just combined (don’t overmix).

4.Fill the Ramekins

  1. Pour the batter evenly into prepared ramekins, filling about ¾ full.

5.Bake

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C (390°F).
  2. Bake for 10–12 minutes until the edges are set but the center is still soft.

6.Rest and Unmold

  1. Let the cakes sit for 1 minute after baking.
  2. Run a knife around the edges and carefully invert onto a plate.

7. Serve

  1. Serve immediately while warm.
  2. Best enjoyed with vanilla ice cream or dusted powdered sugar for contrast.
