BANANA PUDDING

Description

Banana pudding is a classic creamy dessert made with layers of vanilla pudding, fresh banana slices, and vanilla wafers or biscuits. It's sweet, smooth, and comforting, making it a favorite for family gatherings, parties, or an easy make-ahead treat.

Ingredients

For the Pudding

  • 2 cups (500ml) whole milk
  • ½ cup (100g) sugar
  • 3 tbsp cornstarch
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter

For Assembly

  • 3–4 ripe bananas, sliced
  • 200g vanilla wafers (or plain biscuits)
  • 1 cup whipped cream (optional)

For Garnish

  • Crushed vanilla wafers
  • Banana slices
  • Whipped cream

Preparation Steps

1.Make the Pudding

  1. In a saucepan, whisk together sugar and cornstarch.
  2. Gradually add milk while whisking until smooth.
  3. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly.
  4. Once the mixture begins to thicken, remove a small amount and whisk it into the egg yolks.
  5. Slowly pour the egg mixture back into the saucepan while stirring.
  6. Cook for another 1–2 minutes until thick and creamy.
  7. Remove from heat and stir in butter and vanilla extract.
  8. Let cool slightly.

2. Prepare the Bananas

  1. Peel and slice the bananas into thin rounds.
  2. Set aside for layering.

3. Assemble the Pudding

  1. In a serving dish or individual cups:
    • Add a layer of vanilla wafers.
    • Add a layer of banana slices.
    • Spread a layer of pudding.
  2. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used.
  3. Finish with a layer of pudding on top.

4. Chill

  1. Cover and refrigerate for at least 3–4 hours.
  2. For best flavor and texture, chill overnight.

5. Garnish and Serve

  1. Top with whipped cream if desired.
  2. Sprinkle crushed vanilla wafers on top.
  3. Add a few fresh banana slices for decoration.
  4. Serve cold.

Tips:

  • Use ripe bananas with a few brown spots for the best flavor.
  • Assemble shortly before serving if you want the wafers to stay crunchy.
  • For a richer dessert, fold whipped cream into the cooled pudding before layering.
