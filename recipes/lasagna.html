Lasagna Recipe

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian baked pasta dish made with layers of tender pasta sheets, rich meat sauce, creamy cheese, and melted mozzarella. It's hearty, comforting, and perfect for family dinners or special occasions.

Ingredients

For the meat Sauce

  • 500g ground beef
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 800g canned crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried basil

For the Cheese Layer

  • 250g ricotta cheese (or cottage cheese)
  • 1 egg
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese

For Assembly

  • 12 lasagna noodles/sheets
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Extra Parmesan cheese for topping

Preparation Steps

1.Make the Meat Sauce

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pan.
  2. Cook the onion until soft.
  3. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute.
  4. Add ground beef and cook until browned.
  5. Stir in tomatoes, tomato paste, oregano, basil, salt, and pepper.
  6. Simmer for 20–30 minutes.

2.Prepare the Cheese Mixture

  1. In a bowl, combine ricotta cheese, egg, and Parmesan cheese.
  2. Mix until smooth.

3.Cook the Lasagna Sheets

  1. Boil the lasagna noodles according to package instructions.
  2. Drain and set aside.

4.Assemble the Lasagna

  1. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in a baking dish.
  2. Add a layer of noodles.
  3. Spread some cheese mixture.
  4. Add meat sauce.
  5. Sprinkle mozzarella.
  6. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used.
  7. Finish with mozzarella and Parmesan on top.

5.Bake

  1. Preheat oven to 190°C (375°F).
  2. Cover with foil and bake for 25 min
  3. Remove foil and bake for another 15–20 minutes until golden and bubbly.

6.Rest and Serve

  1. Let the lasagna rest for 10–15 minutes before slicing and serving.
